MATCHworks TikTok Tool - Privacy Policy

This application is used solely by its owner (MATCHworks) for personal automation of their own TikTok account. The app does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any third party. It only accesses the profile information (via user.info.basic) and posts video content (via video.publish) of the single connected TikTok account owned by the app's operator. No data is sold or shared with any other party. Access tokens are stored locally and used only to authenticate the owner's own posting actions.
